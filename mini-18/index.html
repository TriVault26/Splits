⛳ Mini 18

Putt-putt tributes to the greatest holes in golf — Augusta, Pebble, Sawgrass, St Andrews and more — with walls, water, windmills and warps.
How to play:
• Drag back from the ball and release to putt (like a slingshot)
• The dotted line previews where the ball will roll and bounce
• Walls bounce · sand slows · water costs a stroke
• Ride the slopes, time the windmill, use the warps
• Sink it in as few strokes as you can — 2 mulligans per round
